Life at Overton

The pace of
the mountain

Life here turns on a few simple things: still water, long trails, a fire at the pavilion, and neighbors who have been coming up the mountain for years. Most owners arrive on Friday and wish it were already Friday again.

Full-bleed still · the timber pavilion and its copper roof
The pavilion

Where the community gathers

Crafted by Blue Ridge Timberwrights from hand-cut timbers and finished with a custom copper roof, the award-winning pavilion sits on the north shore of the lake.

On weekend evenings owners wander down with appetizers and something to pour. Nobody sends an invitation, and nobody needs one. Through the year the pavilion also hosts the community's celebrations, from the Fourth of July cookout to the fall chili cook-off.

Still · the Barn above the second lake
The Barn

A clubhouse in the trees

Overlooking the second lake, the Barn is the year-round heart of the community. A lounge with a reading area for cold evenings, billiards, ping pong and cards upstairs, and a fitness room with commercial-grade Precor equipment.

  • Two FlexCourt tennis courts
  • Practice wall and basketball goal
  • Volleyball court conversion
  • Lounge and game room
Still · trail through hardwoods in autumn
Lakes and trails

Miles of private ground

Two spring-fed lakes for canoeing and fishing, kept quiet with electric motors only. Hiking and biking trails circle both lakes and wind across the property, along with large interior parks with room for a ballgame.
