A private retreat on the Cumberland Plateau

Explore the homesites

High on Long Mountain, at the edge of the Cumberland Plateau, a gated community of bluff and lake view homesites set among two spring-fed lakes and miles of private trail. Ninety minutes from Nashville, and a world apart from it.

GatedTwo spring-fed lakesOwners since 2007

The pavilion

An award-winning gathering place crafted by Blue Ridge Timberwrights on the north shore of the lake.

The water

Electric motors only

Two private, spring-fed lakes kept quiet for canoes, fishing and the sound of nothing much at all.

By the water

Mornings that start on the lake

Lake view homesites look across private, spring-fed water where only electric motors are allowed. Fish from the shore, paddle out before coffee, walk the trail that circles both lakes.

Lake view homesites
Still · bluff edge, long view at golden hour
On the bluff

Evenings at the edge of the plateau

Bluff view homesites sit along the mountain's edge, wooded for privacy and open to the western light. Each is placed to frame the view, with utilities and fiber run underground to the lot line.

Bluff view homesites
Still · timber pavilion, neighbors at dusk
Among neighbors

Weekend evenings at the pavilion

Owners drift down to the pavilion on weekend evenings with something to share. Through the year there is music on the mountain, a fall chili cook-off, and a spring fishing tournament on the lake.

About

Planned to protect the land

The master plan and architectural guidelines were drawn by Chip Webster, an M.I.T.-trained architect with offices in Nantucket, Boston and Vail. Every homesite, road and trail was placed to keep the mountain looking like the mountain.
